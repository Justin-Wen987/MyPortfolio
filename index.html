About me

I like seeing someone get interested in a game I’ve made.

In Forgotten Things, a friend struggled with my mouse-and-keyboard controls. I replaced the mixed inputs with seven keys and tap notes, giving each lane a matching key. When they tried again, they could use the controls without a detailed explanation.
